            Rules, Limits & Sizing Manual

            Stockbee EP Heavy Sizing Philosophy

            • 2x Conviction Risk: Stockbee steps up from baseline 1x risk to 2x risk on true Episodic Pivots with strong catalysts. Here that is earned: Tier 2+, posture YES, all three catalyst checks, and never above the hard risk ceiling.
            • Intraday ORB Entries (Tight Stops): EPs entered on 1-min / 5-min ORB high breakout allow 0.5%-1.5% stop distance, which pushes exposure up. The exposure cap per tier keeps that in check.
            • Institutional Backing: Catalysts attract long-term fund buyers, reducing immediate failure rates.

            Stockbee Selling Rules (Selling Into Strength)

            • 3-5 Day Rule: Most breakouts run for 3 to 5 days before pulling back. Sell 1/3 to 1/2 of your position into strength on Days 3-5.
            • Pay Yourself First: Lock in partial profits at +1R to +2R to bring your effective risk on the trade to zero.
            • EP Runner Rule: For Episodic Pivots, trail the remaining piece using the 10 EMA or Previous Day's Low to capture 20%-50%+ home run moves.

            12 Always Rules

            1. Always trade for magnitude, not duration.
            2. Always operate with Situational Awareness.
            3. Always buy on the first day of breakout - earlier the better.
            4. Always look for tightness or a negative day in the pre-breakout.
            5. Always look for young setups and moves.
            6. Always look for a catalyst.
            7. Always think: "who is going to buy after me, and whom can I sell this to?"
            8. Always sell into strength and move stops to protect profits.
            9. Always look for the opportunity to size up on fund-quality plays.
            10. Always develop new edges and variations - build a larger playbook.
            11. Always study 20% and 50% moves.
            12. Edge turnaround and rotation - edges decay; be ready to rotate to what's working now.
